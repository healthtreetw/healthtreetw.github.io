<section class="about-section">
  <h2>我們的歷程（Our Roadmap）</h2>
  <p>從 2023 年 6 月的病友見面交流開始，約有 50 位朋友在線下相聚；當天活動加上線上參與，約有上百位朋友一起交流。從那時起，台灣的 MM 病友與家屬們逐漸形成自發性的連結，每年固定舉辦數場見面、茶敘、年末聚會或主題分享。</p>
  <p>這些活動不是大型組織的正式服務，而是由病友、家屬與朋友互相扶持、彼此邀請而累積起來的交流空間。歡迎大家留意後續見面訊息，也鼓勵有需要的朋友一起參與，透過面對面的對話認識彼此、交換經驗、分享資源。</p>

    <h3>About the MM Information Platform</h3>
    <p>This website records information sharing, local gatherings, and mutual support among people living with multiple myeloma, family members, and friends in Taiwan.</p>
    <p>We aim to organize public educational resources, international updates, and local activity records in a clear format that people can revisit. The content is for information sharing only and should not be used as a substitute for medical diagnosis, treatment advice, or individual medical decision-making.</p>

    <h3>Our Roadmap</h3>
    <p>Beginning with the June 2023 patient meet-up, about 50 friends joined in person, and roughly one hundred people participated when online interaction was included. Since then, MM patients, families, and friends in Taiwan have gradually built a self-initiated support network, holding several in-person gatherings, tea meetings, year-end meetups, and topic-based sharing sessions each year.</p>
    <p>These activities are not a formal institutional service. They have grown from patients, family members, and friends supporting one another, inviting one another, and creating space for conversation. We welcome people to follow future gathering announcements and encourage those who need support to join, meet others, exchange experiences, and share resources.</p>

    <h3>Milestones</h3>
    <p><strong>2023:</strong> April - 1st Patient Meet &amp; Greet; June - 2nd Patient Meet &amp; Greet; October - Immunology Speech &amp; CAR-T Sharing; November - 3rd AMN Patient Forum and Taipei 1st Year-end Meet.</p>
    <p><strong>2024:</strong> February - HealthTree TW Connect Group website and translated information sharing; October - 4th AMN Patient Forum; November - Taipei 2nd Year-end Meet.</p>
    <p><strong>2025:</strong> January - Hsin-Chu 1st Meet &amp; Greet; May, June, August, and September - Taipei 3rd to 6th Meet &amp; Greet; October - 5th AMN Patient Forum; November - Taipei 3rd Year-end Meet and North Taiwan 1st Meet &amp; Greet.</p>
